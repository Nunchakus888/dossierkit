# DossierKit Content Voice

This repository publishes outward-facing career material for recruiters, hiring managers, CEOs, partners, and senior technical evaluators.

Use a public, professional voice:

- Write as external presentation copy, not internal notes or self-review.
- Prefer concise active statements such as "负责", "主导", "建设", "沉淀", "交付", "owned", "led", "built", and "delivered".
- Avoid first-person pronouns unless a long-form narrative explicitly requires them.
- Avoid incomplete-status language such as "待补", "后续补", "当前短板", "真实项目待补充", "for now", or "to be filled".
- Avoid self-justifying language such as "不是 demo", "不是算法负责人", "不需要包装", or "不作为主线". Convert it into positive scope statements.
- Avoid addressing the reader as an interviewer or evaluator. Use "适合沟通", "可展开讨论", "evidence includes", or "discussion areas" instead.
- Keep one strategic positioning: enterprise Agent platform architecture. Past Alibaba and SenseTime experience should support this positioning, not create scattered target directions.
- Sanitized metrics are acceptable; missing metrics should be omitted from public pages rather than called out as missing.

Before committing content changes, scan public content for placeholder or internal wording: `demo`, `example`, `待补`, `后续`, `短板`, `面试官`, `不需要`, `不作为`, `not a demo`, `to be filled`.

## Understanding-first notes

Research, learning, and engineering notes follow the canonical publish-note Skill rather than the career-presentation guidance above. Apply STE-inspired clear prose, define essential terms on first use, preserve causal and evidence boundaries, and ground difficult mechanisms in a labeled example or relevant failure case. Choose media by what the reader must understand; keep simple prose simple, long Mermaid flows vertical, and computational HTML or video opt-in and separately reviewed. Review whether the reader can explain, transfer, identify limits, and verify the argument. Do not add personal career advice to technical notes. The Skill and its references remain the source of truth; the build regenerates the MCP catalog and plugin package from them.

## Public content is not the authoring SOP

Apply the built-in prompt and Skill silently. Public articles serve readers who do not know our conversation or publishing workflow. Keep internal prompt updates, generation SOP, review checklists, tool/repository/deployment notes, and future-generation promises outside published content, including folded appendices. Requests to publish learning and update the SOP produce two separate deliverables. Put process rules in the prompt/Skill and completion details in the reply. Mention DossierKit in a public article only when the user explicitly requests that product as the subject. The 30-second layer and skim test are internal aids, not headings or compulsory sections to copy into articles. Useful reader-facing prompt examples remain appropriate when prompting itself is the subject. Preserve evidence and material limitations in the public explanation.

## Public UI actions

Use the shared `IconAction` for familiar utility actions (share, copy, language, contact, download, print, back, zoom, close). Keep utility chrome icon-first, neutral, and quiet. Remove redundant action headings and explanatory prose. Retain meaningful navigation labels and content titles where an icon would be ambiguous. Every icon-only action needs a localized accessible name and hover title, visible keyboard focus, and a 44px touch target. Success feedback may be a temporary check mark with a live-region announcement; keep a usable fallback when an operation fails. Do not change collection semantics, metrics, content, or destinations during visual-only changes.

Language switches resolve published counterparts by translationKey or slug at build time; missing translations keep the reader on the current page with a compact choice of original or target-language index. Keep an actual, valid fallback href for no-JS and modified clicks. Share utilities align right. The site wordmark uses the existing Pacifico signature face; do not add a second font library. Mermaid previews preserve intrinsic aspect ratio and never upscale narrow diagrams to a fixed minimum width.

The header and footer slogans share the `signature-wordmark` script treatment; keep normal content and utility text unstyled. Notes have a single title link: no duplicate trailing-arrow action or whole-row click handler. Browser tab identity is the self-contained connected George Ge signature in public/favicon.svg, with committed PNG/ICO fallbacks generated by scripts/ui/generate-brand-icons.mjs. Keep font files out of the repository and generated artifacts.

## Note visual authoring

Apply the canonical `plugins/dossierkit-notes/skills/publish-note/SKILL.md` and its visual-authoring reference. Privately sketch a scan-first 30-second layer: direct thesis, 3–5 key takeaways, and at most one primary diagram or table when it materially reduces decoding work. Turn that sketch into natural topic-specific content, not a duplicated summary and checklist. A reader scanning headings, bold lead-ins and structured elements should still recover the argument. Move subject-relevant secondary detail behind progressive disclosure or later sections instead of building a wall of prose; internal publishing instructions never belong in these sections.

For content that genuinely benefits from structured explanation, prefer **Mermaid > Table > SVG > Text**. Use diagrams to reveal otherwise hard-to-follow branches, interactions, or abstractions, not to repaint clear prose or tables. Ordinary prose needs no extra image, and there is no body-image quota. Keep the topic-specific Open Graph cover independent of body visuals: preserve it during body simplification and verify its generated public PNG plus sharing metadata.

Keep favicon backgrounds transparent, preserve connected-letter counters at 16/32px, and regenerate PNG/ICO fallbacks plus versioned links when artwork changes. Back-link arrow ink aligns with the article left edge while retaining the shared 44px touch target.
