## Positioning

This is not a standalone career direction. It supports the enterprise Agent platform architect positioning by showing how production traces become a continuous quality improvement system.

## Loop

The platform needs structured traces and a clear badcase taxonomy: wrong tool selection, wrong parameters, poor task decomposition, refusal policy errors, and missing business rules.

These records can feed three paths:

1. Evaluation samples for prompt, tool schema, and model release gates.
2. Product improvement through workflow gaps, rule gaps, and human fallback design.
3. Data construction through chosen/rejected preference pairs for later training or policy optimization.

## Senior Value

The point is not claiming a separate title. The point is proving platform ownership: connecting production behavior, evaluation, model improvement, and business outcomes.
