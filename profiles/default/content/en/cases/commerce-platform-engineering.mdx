## Overview

At Alibaba Taobao and Local Services, worked on high-traffic cross-platform web engineering, micro-frontends, ad attribution, logging SDKs, JsBridge, and performance optimization across live interaction, content scenarios, ad delivery, and merchant analytics.

This stage built a foundation in platform abstraction, performance governance, cross-team execution, and commercial-loop engineering under high-traffic business conditions.

## Taobao Live

In Taobao Live, the web interaction layer work covered:

1. Evolved isolated Weex components into H5 micro-frontend orchestration with unified layout, routing, event bus, and communication contracts.
2. Supported Weex, H5, and multiple web technology stacks during architecture migration.
3. Used SSR, HTTP2/HTTP1 resource strategies, and rendering optimization to protect first-screen experience in complex business and weak-network environments.
4. Related performance targets reached FCP < 1.5s class experience, close to the original Weex implementation.

## Local Services Commercialization

In Alibaba Local Services commercial promotion, the work centered on ad delivery, order attribution, and merchant operating data:

1. Designed a shell / iframe micro-frontend architecture to standardize sub-application communication, routing, static resource loading, and deployment.
2. Built Vue and React scaffolds, cross-platform JsBridge, ad logging SDK, and reusable component libraries.
3. Supported H5 delivery and mini-program development across Ele.me, Taobao, Alipay, WeChat, and related channels.
4. Contributed to the commercial loop from user ordering and ad billing to order attribution and merchant business analysis.
5. Built SSR-based marketing-page performance improvements; related scenarios improved FCP by about 40% and reached FCP < 1.8s.

## Platform Value

This stage contributed three practical engineering foundations:

- Reliability and performance thinking under high-traffic business conditions.
- Platform abstraction across teams, technology stacks, and client containers.
- Logging, attribution, SDK, and commercial-loop awareness, which transfer directly to Agent trace, tool audit, and usage/cost governance.
